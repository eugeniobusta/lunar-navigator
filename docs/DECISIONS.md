# Decisions (append one line each: date-time, who, decision, why)

- Sat 09:00, team: Static web app (React/TS/Vite) + Python precompute. Why: demo must not depend on venue Wi-Fi or a server.
- Sat 09:00, team: One global ephemeris file (Sun/Earth in MOON_ME) + per-site horizon masks; browser computes alt/az. Why: any site, instant, tiny data.
- Sat 09:00, team: Skyfield + DE421 + moon_pa_de421; validate against JPL Horizons. Why: offline, documented lunar frame.
- Sat 09:00, team: LOLA LDEM_75S_120M for horizons (232 MB), 150 km range, observer 2 m. Why: covers all candidate regions + far terrain; same resolution as PGDA validation maps.
- Sat 09:00, team: App date range 2026-11-01 to 2029-12-31, hourly. Why: covers Griffin-1, Blue Moon MK1, Artemis IV (early 2028).
- Sat 09:00, team: Sunlit / Earth-visible = disc CENTRE above horizon; power uses visible disc fraction. Why: standard and explainable.
- Sat 09:00, team: d3 is the only chart library. Why: one library, full control.
- Sat 09:10, Eugenio: Generated JSON in web/public/data/ IS committed (ephemeris ~2-3 MB, horizons small); raw data/ is not. Why: Vercel deploys straight from the repo.
- Sat 09:10, Eugenio: React pinned to 18 (Vite template ships 19). Why: CLAUDE.md stack.
- Sat 09:10, Eugenio: Unverified mission sites (griffin1, bluemoon1, change7) left out of sites.json until coordinates are confirmed. Why: never show a guessed site as exact.
- Sat 09:30, Eugenio: Cloud Claude builds web/ and firmware/; Eugenio's local Claude Code (Fable 5.2) builds the Python pipeline. Why: the cloud workspace cannot reach NASA/JPL hosts; humans focus on hardware, validation review, story and pitch.
- Sat 09:30, Claude: computeMetrics takes an optional stepH (default 1 h). Why: Series carries no sample spacing; the contract signature computeMetrics(s) still works.
- Sat 09:30, Claude: Window Finder score = mean of Sun% and Earth% over the best window that day; every landing hour is tried. Why: simple, explainable to judges.
- Sat 09:30, Claude: "hoursBelowZero" = hours where the battery could not cover the load. Why: reaching exactly 0 Wh with the load met is not a failure.
- Sat 10:00, team: Team name is "Claude's Plan". Used in the README, the app footer and the submission.
- Sat 10:00, Claude (local): Sun semi-diameter uses the IAU nominal solar radius 695,700 km; ephemeris code lives in engine/ephem.py so the long-run validation reuses it. Why: one method for app data and validation.
- Sat 10:15, Claude (local): Horizon marching keeps the SCIENCE.md step (1.5 % growth). Why: measured against a 30x finer march it under-reads the horizon by at most 0.08 deg (mean 0.004 deg) at our sites.
- Sat 10:15, Claude (local): Horizon lower bound is the curvature dip of the observer's own height, not a fixed -5 deg. Why: mmo01 stands at 6.4 km and correctly sees down to -5.06 deg.
- Sat 10:15, Claude (local): mmp06 and nr208 kept at their tabulated coordinates although the DEM puts them in local hollows (horizon above 1-3 deg all round). Why: sites.json belongs to Eugenio; flagged for the coordinate check against Wueller et al.
- Sat 10:30, Claude (local): PGDA comparison reports centre-of-disc (what the app shows) and, separately, PGDA's own definitions and a 0 m observer. Why: the 2 m mast and the any-part rule explain the gaps and should be visible, not hidden.
- Sat 11:15, Claude (local): Test files are excluded from the app's tsc build; a root vercel.json builds web/ for Git deploys. Why: Vercel uploads only web/ from the CLI, so a test importing ../tests/fixtures broke the production build.
- Sat 11:40, Claude (local): Pitch numbers come from scripts/pitch_numbers.py only; the mma01 landing-day count was cross-checked against the app's findWindows (same 32 days). Why: stage numbers must match what the demo shows.
- Sat 12:10, Eugenio: INTERFACES.md section 8 added (3D terrain export, 1024 x 1024, 220 km half-width). Why: data for Ahmed's 3D view; each pixel is an area mean, so it is for display, not per-site numbers.
- Sat 12:40, Eugenio: Code is MIT, our derived data CC BY 4.0, source data keeps its providers' terms (DATA_LICENSE.md quotes each terms page). web/public/data/index.json lists every published file; a test keeps it in step.
- Sat 11:00, Chinh: Window Finder score changed to the mean of normalised safety margins (Sun, Earth, longest dark; 0 = only just passes, 1 = perfect), replacing the mean of Sun% and Earth%. Why: planners need slack for failures (e.g. a degraded panel); the score now depends on the mission thresholds. findWindows moved to windows.ts (prefix sums + sparse table), with a Web Worker in web/src/workers/.
- Sat 13:00, Eugenio: INTERFACES.md section 9 added (per-site terrain tiles, 512 x 512 at the DEM's native 120 m, cut on the DEM pixel grid so nothing is resampled). Why: 1:1 terrain, heat maps and in-browser horizons for the VR view (xr/).
- Sat 13:00, Claude (VR): each site tile also carries far.u16 (600 m, +/- 153.6 km). Why: with only terrain/south_pole.* as far field, Malapert's horizon was up to 1.87 deg wrong, because it looks north over 120-150 km of terrain beyond the 220 km south-pole grid; with far.u16 all 10 sites agree with horizons/<id>.json to 0.027 deg.
- Sat 14:30, Eugenio: "Earthrise" redesign: palette in web/src/styles/tokens.css, one meaning per colour (sunlight = Sun and power, earth blue = Earth and radio link, eclipse = dark or no link, lichen = OK). Buttons, links and focus rings are bone, never blue. MoonBox tab removed (firmware and web/src/lib/serial kept). Charts draw at real pixel size so text stays readable on a phone. "North points towards Earth" is shown only for polar sites, because it is false at Apollo 11 or on the far side.
- Sat 13:30, Claude (VR): xr/ is a separate Vite app (IWSDK 1.1.0, its own package.json) served at /xr/ by the same Vercel site; it reads /data/ and imports web/src/lib/analysis directly. Why: the website stays React-only and light; one copy of the data and of the maths.
- Sat 16:00, Eugenio: Landing criteria (docs/criteria.md). Hard limits are NASA's "Key Artemis III Landing Requirements" (NTRS 20240009596): 6 deg from the pole, landing slope < 10 deg, EVA slope < 20 deg within 2 km, Earth visible (NASA's 25 % mask), lit for the stay, 100 m x 100 m. Our own choices, each marked "team choice" in docs/criteria.csv: 120 m slope baseline, 50 % walkable ground, a 25 % sunlight screen calibrated on 243 modelled points, and the score weights.
- Sat 16:00, Eugenio: INTERFACES.md section 10 added (suitability files for the "Where to land" tab and the VR view). Score grids carry pass/fail and score in one byte: 0..100 pass, 101..201 fail, 255 no data.
- Sat 16:00, Eugenio: Temperature comes from Diviner's Polar Resource Product (a thermal model, ~0.5 km mesh, one 605 MB file), not the 240 m product, which would need about 20 GB of downloads. Why: one honest, documented number beats a missing column.
- Sat 16:00, Eugenio: Research notes with source quotes are kept in docs/research/. docs/missions.csv lists 41 lunar landing attempts; Luna 25 is "crashed" and SLIM "tipped over" (both debatable, see the notes).
- Sat 17:00, Eugenio: Playwright smoke test (web/e2e/smoke.spec.ts) runs in CI on the built site: every tab must open with content and no console errors, and the Window Finder must return a calendar. New dev dependency @playwright/test in web/. Why: a teammate's PR can no longer silently break another tab.
- Sat 14:30, Claude (VR): the VR sky shows real stars (Yale Bright Star Catalogue to V 3.5, rotated with the Moon's real orientation), INTERFACES.md section 11. Why: nothing in the VR view is invented; CDS (Hipparcos) was unreachable from the venue, the BSC JSON on GitHub was.
- Sat 14:30, Claude (VR): the VR sky shows real stars (Yale Bright Star Catalogue to V 3.5, rotated with the Moon's real orientation), INTERFACES.md section 10. Why: nothing in the VR view is invented; CDS (Hipparcos) was unreachable from the venue, the BSC JSON on GitHub was.
- Sat 15:00, Claude (VR): terrain and sky write a logarithmic depth into the far half of the depth range; ordinary objects use the near half. Why: one scene spans 2 m to 300 km, and a standard depth buffer put ridges 100 km away in the wrong order (about 1 deg of skyline error).
- Sat 15:00, Claude (VR): the VR floating panel is a canvas on a plane with the app's own ray test, not UIKitML. Why: same palette and font as the laptop HUD, one code path for trigger and pinch.
- Sat 14:17, Claude (VR): with no `t` in the URL the VR opens a site or zone at the next hour the whole Sun is above its skyline (computeSeries), not at "now"; `demo=1` opens Orbit with the Goldilocks map. Why: a demo that starts in the dark shows nothing.
- Sat 14:17, Claude (VR): a zone outside every 120 m site tile opens on a tile interpolated from the ~430 m south-pole grid, labelled as such, with no slope map. Why: every zone in a top-10 list must be reachable under any priorities (11 of the 28 zones in the four presets' top 10 have no site tile), without adding tiles to the repository.
- Sat 14:17, Claude (VR): the tour's last stop plays 2 days at 4 hours per second, not at "100x". Why: the speed buttons are hours per second, and at 100 the two days last half a second.
- Sat 14:17, Claude (VR): automatic quality lowers detail when the mean frame time over 72 frames exceeds 13.9 ms by more than 5 %, and never raises it during a session. Why: a steady 72 Hz headset reports 13.89 ms with jitter, and switching back and forth is worse than staying low.

INTERFACES.md section 11 added; no section-10 file changed. Presets Power / Radio link / Ice science are a team choice. Layer rounding is nudged by one step in 2.65 % of cells so the balanced weights reproduce the published scores exactly.
